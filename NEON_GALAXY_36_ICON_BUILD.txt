NEON GALAXY — 36 ICON PROJECT

This project contains 36 app icon mappings for Projectivy Launcher.
The seven additions are Rakuten TV, Projectivy Blueprint, Arcticons, Projectivy Weather, Overflight, Shizuku and ADB AppControl.

Build with:
  ./gradlew :app:assembleDebug

Expected output:
  app/build/outputs/apk/debug/*.apk

The appfilter.xml and drawable.xml mappings are included in both assets and res/xml.
